About

Academic Biography

Abeer Alsadoon received her Ph.D. from the University of Technology, Baghdad, and has more than 25 years of academic experience in teaching, research, supervision, and academic leadership. Her research interests include Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Health Informatics, Augmented and Mixed Reality, and Computing Education. She has published 147 journal papers and 111 peer-reviewed international conference papers, including IEEE publications, and received four Best Paper Awards. She has edited five scholarly books and 12 international conference proceedings published by Springer. Abeer has received 20 awards and recognitions for excellence in teaching and research from Australian institutions. In 2019, she gained national recognition as one of five finalists across Australia for the prestigious Australian Women's Agenda Leadership Awards in the category of Emerging Female Leader in the Government and Public Sector.

Abeer Alsadoon received her Ph.D. from the University of Technology, Baghdad, and has more than 25 years of academic experience in teaching, research, supervision, and academic leadership. Her research interests include Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Health Informatics, Augmented and Mixed Reality, and Computing Education. She has published 147 journal papers and 111 peer-reviewed international conference papers, including IEEE publications, and received four Best Paper Awards. She has edited five scholarly books and 12 international conference proceedings published by Springer. Abeer has received 20 awards and recognitions for excellence in teaching and research from Australian institutions. In 2019, she gained national recognition as one of five finalists across Australia for the prestigious Australian Women's Agenda Leadership Awards in the category of Emerging Female Leader in the Government and Public Sector. She served as Conference Chair and Proceedings Editor of the International Conference on Health Informatics and Medical Systems (HIMS'22–HIMS'26), Las Vegas, USA, and as Program Chair of CITISIA'20–CITISIA'25. She has also served as Program Chair and Proceedings Editor of ICR'22–ICR'26, ACR'23–ACR'26, and BAI'26, with forthcoming appointments for BCR'27 (Nice, France), ACR'27 (Dublin, Ireland), ICR'27 (Barcelona, Spain), and HIMS’27 (Las Vegas, USA). Abeer is a Senior Member of IEEE. She is currently an Associate Professor at the Education Centre of Australia (ECA) – Asia Pacific International College (APIC). Previously, she served as Dean of Scholarship and Research, Associate Dean, and IT Discipline Leader at Kent Institute Australia, held a sessional academic appointment at Western Sydney University (WSU), and held academic positions at Charles Sturt University (CSU), including Adjunct Associate Professor and IT Course Coordinator.
