Welcome to my world

Hi, I’m Abeer Alsadoon
Associate Professor PhD (Soft. Eng.) IEEE Senior Member ACM Senior Member

Associate Professor Dr Abeer Alsadoon brings more than 25 years of experience in higher-education teaching, research, curriculum leadership and academic governance. At Education Centre of Australia–Asia Pacific International College, she teaches across delivery modes, supervises higher degree research, and contributes to curriculum, quality, compliance and institutional leadership. Her research spans software engineering, artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, augmented and mixed reality, health informatics and computing education. She is an IEEE Senior Member and ACM Senior Member, an international conference leader and proceedings editor, and a nationally recognised finalist in the 2019 Australian Women’s Agenda Leadership Awards.
